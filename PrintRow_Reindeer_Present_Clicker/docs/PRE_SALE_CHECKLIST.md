**Status: NOT approved for sale. NOT safety-approved.** This is a checklist for physical prototypes.
Tick an item only on the basis of a real print. Record the date, printer, filament, settings and who tested it.

## 1. Switch fit (test kit first, then 3 full builds)
- [ ] Switch type confirmed and recorded (brand, model, 3/5-pin): ____________
- [ ] Plate gauge that holds best: 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 → `SW_CUTOUT` = ____ mm (default 14.00)
- [ ] Switch snaps into the product box plate, both clips engaged, no rocking
- [ ] Switch does not pull out when the head is pulled off (clips hold)
- [ ] Switch can be removed with an MX switch puller through the opening, without damage
- [ ] Pins clear the pocket floor (switch sits flush on the plate)

## 2. Head movement
- [ ] Socket gauge that grips best: 1 / 2 / 3 → `STEM_SLOT_W` = ____ mm (default 1.35)
- [ ] Head presses on square and seats fully; no visible tilt at rest
- [ ] Full click on every press; head returns fully every time
- [ ] No rubbing or catching on the box, rim, ribbon caps or legs, with centred presses, edge presses (4 sides) and corner presses
- [ ] Visible gap round the head at rest ≈ 1–2 mm all round (digital: ≥ 1.06 mm to box, ≥ 1.55 mm to legs)
- [ ] Head cannot be wiggled into contact with the legs or rim with normal finger force
- [ ] Head pull-off force: comes off by hand, but does not fall off when the toy is turned upside down and shaken

## 3. Leg strength (fixed legs)
- [ ] Legs click into the rim slots; seated flat on the rim top and front face
- [ ] Legs do not move when the head is pressed hard
- [ ] Leg pull-out: does not come out with a firm finger pull (record: by hand / with a ____ N pull gauge)
- [ ] Hoof push-down / side push: no cracking at the tab root (5 samples)
- [ ] Drop test from 1 m onto a hard floor, 5 orientations: legs stay attached (record any failures)

## 4. Antler and ear strength (printed upright, cross-layer)
- [ ] Antler side push with a finger: no crack or white stress mark (5 samples)
- [ ] Antler break test on a sacrificial print: record approximate force / failure location
- [ ] Ear edge push: no crack
- [ ] Drop test (as above): antlers / ears intact
- [ ] If antlers fail: consider 3 walls / 25 % infill / higher temperature, or the antler redesign option (thicker roots)

## 5. Repeated clicking
- [ ] 1,000 presses (manual or jig): no loosening of head, switch or legs; no new rubbing marks
- [ ] 10,000 presses on at least one unit (jig recommended): record condition
- [ ] Inspect the cross socket for cracking or loosening after the cycles

## 6. Detachable parts (child safety: size them all)
List each part that can come off **without tools** and check it against a small-parts cylinder (31.7 mm Ø test gauge):

| Part | Detachable by hand? | Fits small-parts cylinder? | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Head (≈ 31 × 47 × 42 mm) | Yes (by design) | Expected no, check | Exposes the switch |
| **Switch** (≈ 15.6 mm cube) | Only with a puller (check) | **Yes: small part** | Metal spring and contacts inside |
| Legs (≈ 7 × 11 × 14 mm each) | Designed not to (check) | **Yes if detached** | Snap barbs |
| Bow (≈ 27 × 16 × 5 mm) | Trapped by the legs (check) | **Likely yes if detached** | Dovetail |
| Ribbon cage | Flex off (check) | No (large) | Thin straps could snap |
| Antler/ear fragments if broken | n/a | Yes | See section 4 |

- [ ] Each "designed not to detach" part survives a firm pull and twist without coming off
- [ ] Broken fragments from destructive tests checked for sharp edges / points

## 11. Packaging

- [ ] Includes warning label and care note (see section 12)
- [ ] Drop-tested in its packaging (1 m, 3 drops)

## 12. Product-specific safety review (to complete before listing)
- [ ] **Age grading decided** and consistent across listing, packaging and photos. The existing PrintRow clickers use "8+ / not suitable for under 3 / contains small parts".
- [ ] **Small parts:** the switch, legs and bow are small parts if detached, and the head comes off by design. Treat the product as **not suitable for children under 3** and check the current Australian mandatory requirements for toys for children up to and including 36 months (ACCC / productsafety.gov.au) before marketing it in any way that could appeal to young children. A cute reindeer is attractive to toddlers, so wording, photos and category matter.
- [ ] Warning text on the listing and packaging (e.g. "WARNING: CHOKING HAZARD – small parts. Not for children under 3 years.")
- [ ] No batteries, magnets, lights or electronics (confirmed by design). If any are added later, battery and magnet rules apply.
- [ ] Sharp edges / points: inspect the antler tips, hoof cleft, cage strap ends and bow V-notches on real prints
- [ ] Material: PLA. Note "keep away from heat (cars, heaters)" and do not describe it as food-safe.
- [ ] Mechanical keyboard switch: confirm the supplier and that it is a standard consumer component
- [ ] Decide whether a formal toy-safety assessment is needed for the chosen market and age grade; keep the evidence with the product records
- [ ] Do **not** describe the product as "safety tested" or "safety approved" unless that testing has actually been done
